Design direction — “instrument, not dashboard”

The product is a device. The shipped UI is a web dashboard that happens to contain one. Here the device is the only filled surface on the page, and everything else is air, hairlines and tabular data.

  • Archivo, not the system stack System fonts render SF Pro on macOS — the most generic face available, and the “no decision” decision. Archivo is a grotesque with tight apertures that survives 10px uppercase. Vendored locally, so runtime network calls stay at zero.
  • The device is the only box Every other border is gone. That single filled surface is what makes the screen read as the hero — no inflation required.
  • One rule, reused A 1px horizontal divider is the only line in the file. No enclosing outlines, no internal grid dividers, no bordered inputs. No card shadows.
  • Controls are surfaces, not outlines A filled plate with no border and no offset shadow. Pressing goes darker, because pressing pushes in. No neo-brutalist offset.
  • Panels moved below to buy columns Full width is only worth taking if it buys something. Cartridge specs and key bindings become two or three columns instead of one long list.
  • Dark is Stone, not midnight #2E2B26 has headroom in both directions — plates raise, wells recess. Near-black gives you one direction only.
  • The shell inverts, the well does not Dark mode turns the body to grey plastic #8E887C, what a real DMG was. The LCD well stays dark in both, because a recess is a recess.
  • Clay, not hotter vermilion #D2673A at 61% saturation. Saturated warm on mid-grey shimmers on thin rules and gets tiring over an hour.
  • Every number is Plex Mono, tabular A changing FPS readout never reflows its neighbours.
  • The claim is underlined, not boxed The emphasis survives the removal of every box; the badge does not.
